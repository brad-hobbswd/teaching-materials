A Connected Process

From Children's Curiosity to Your Next Step

Curriculum is not a binder of disconnected activities. It is a responsive cycle that changes as you learn more about the children in your care.

1. NoticeListen, watch, and identify interests, questions, strengths, and needs.
2. PlanChoose a focus, set intentions, and prepare materials and experiences.
3. EngageExplore alongside children and respond to their ideas as learning unfolds.
4. DocumentCapture meaningful examples of children's words, actions, and strategies.
5. ReflectUse what you noticed to adapt, extend, or choose the next experience.
Keep the pathways distinct: Curriculum supports planning and teaching. School Readiness provides readiness and framework guidance; Behavior & Inclusion supports individual access and behavior; the Resource Library houses practical tools and printables; and Teacher & Family Support provides professional and family resources.
