A Repeatable Process

The Curriculum Planning Cycle

Effective curriculum is not a one-time checklist. It is a continuous process of noticing, planning, engaging, documenting, and reflecting.

1

Notice

Observe children's play, interests, conversations, questions, and emerging skills.

2

Plan

Identify goals, select materials, and prepare meaningful learning opportunities.

3

Engage

Interact with children, ask open-ended questions, and extend their thinking.

4

Document

Record observations, conversations, work samples, and evidence of learning.

5

Reflect

Consider what children learned and determine what to introduce or adapt next.

Remember: Follow children's interests while intentionally connecting experiences to developmental goals, individual needs, and your program's curriculum framework.
