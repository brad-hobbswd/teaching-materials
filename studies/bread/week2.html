🥣 Week 2: Ingredients & Measuring

Children investigate ingredients, tools, quantities, and the sequence of preparing a bread recipe.

Investigation Focus

Measure

Compare cups, spoons, and amounts while exploring simple recipe measurements.

Mix

Combine ingredients and observe how separate materials become a mixture.

Sequence

Practice putting recipe steps in order and using picture or teacher-made sequence cards.

Document

Draw ingredients, record observations, and use new food and measurement vocabulary.
