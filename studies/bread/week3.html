🤲 Week 3: Making Dough

Children explore mixing, kneading, shaping, texture, and the changes that happen as dough is prepared.

Investigation Focus

Mix

Observe how ingredients combine and change as they are mixed.

Knead

Explore movement, texture, pressure, and the physical changes in dough.

Shape

Use dough or safe classroom materials to make different shapes and forms.

Observe

Talk about how dough looks and feels before and after preparation.
