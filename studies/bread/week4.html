🌎 Week 4: Bread Around the World

Children discover bread traditions, food experiences, and cultural connections from communities around the world.

Investigation Focus

Discover

Explore different breads and learn that families and communities have many food traditions.

Compare

Compare shapes, ingredients, textures, and ways breads are served.

Share

Invite children and families to share stories, recipes, and favorite bread experiences.

Celebrate

Create a class display, recipe collection, artwork, or tasting reflection to close the study.
