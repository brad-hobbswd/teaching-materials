🍞 Week 1: Discovering Bread

Children explore different breads, ingredients, family traditions, and how bread connects people and cultures.

Investigation Focus

Explore

Look at different breads and talk about shapes, colors, smells, textures, and familiar foods.

Investigate

Compare ingredients and discuss what children notice before bread is prepared.

Connect

Invite families to share favorite breads, recipes, and food traditions.

Create

Draw, sculpt, or collage different kinds of bread using classroom materials.
