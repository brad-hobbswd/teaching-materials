💡 Week 4: Engineer, Test & Share

Children bring their ideas together in a final design challenge and explain what they discovered.

← Week 3Back to Study
CULMINATING CHALLENGE

Build Something That Works

Invite children to plan a pathway or tunnel system, test it, make changes, and share their engineering thinking.

📝 Plan

Draw or describe the design before building.

🔧 Build & Test

Construct the system and test whether the intended object can move through it.

🗣️ Share

Children explain what worked, what changed, and what they would try next.
