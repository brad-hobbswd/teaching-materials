🏖️ Week 1: Discovering Sand

Children investigate what sand is, how it feels, where it comes from, and how it can be explored through sensory and science experiences.

Back to Sand StudyWeek 2 →
WEEK ONE

Explore • Observe • Describe

Build vocabulary and curiosity through hands-on sand investigations.

🖐️ Feel the Sand

Compare dry sand, coarse sand, and fine sand. Invite children to describe texture.

🔍 Observe the Grains

Use magnifiers to examine individual grains and notice color, shape, and size.

🗣️ Build Vocabulary

Practice words such as grain, texture, smooth, rough, dry, wet, scoop, and pour.

🏖️ Where Does Sand Come From?

Explore beaches, deserts, rocks, and natural materials that become sand over time.
