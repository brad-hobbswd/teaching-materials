Study Overview

The Trees Study encourages children to observe, question, compare, measure, create, and discover the important role trees play in our environment.

Four Week Investigation

Each week builds children's understanding through exploration, observation, discussion, and hands on learning.

Week 1

Getting to Know Trees

Children begin observing trees, identifying their parts, and sharing what they already know.

  • Tree walks
  • Observing bark
  • Leaf collecting
  • Nature journals
  • Tree vocabulary
Open Week →
Week 2

How Trees Grow

Children investigate roots, seeds, leaves, water, sunlight, and seasons.

  • Planting seeds
  • Growth observations
  • Measuring trees
  • Comparing leaves
  • Weather connections
Open Week →
Week 3

Trees and Living Things

Explore animals, insects, birds, nests, habitats, and food that trees provide.

  • Bird watching
  • Animal homes
  • Insect investigations
  • Pinecones
  • Nature art
Open Week →
Week 4

Why Trees Matter

Children discover how trees help people, animals, and our planet.

  • Community trees
  • Wood products
  • Protecting nature
  • Recycling paper
  • Celebration project
Open Week →

Teacher Observation Ideas

Use these prompts while children explore trees, leaves, bark, seeds, habitats, and outdoor materials.

🔎

Observation

What does the child notice about the tree, leaf, bark, branch, or seed?

🗣️

Language

Does the child use tree vocabulary to describe what they see, feel, or discover?

🧩

Problem Solving

How does the child compare, sort, measure, investigate, or test an idea?

🌱

Scientific Thinking

Does the child ask questions, make predictions, and notice changes over time?

✋

Motor Skills

Observe carrying, collecting, building, arranging, digging, and manipulating natural materials.

🤝

Social Learning

Notice how children share materials, cooperate, communicate, and participate with others.

Family Engagement

Simple ways families can continue the Trees Study beyond the classroom.

🏡

Tree Hunt at Home

Invite families to find a tree near home and notice its bark, leaves, branches, and shade.

📷

Share a Photo

Invite families to share a photo of a favorite tree or outdoor discovery.

📖

Read Together

Explore a tree or nature book together at home or at the local library.
