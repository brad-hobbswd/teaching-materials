Trees Study

Week 3: Trees and Living Things

Explore animals, insects, birds, nests, habitats, and the food and shelter that trees provide.

Back to Trees StudyExplore Activities

Weekly Investigations

Use observation, conversation, movement, art, and outdoor exploration to help children investigate the living things connected to trees.

🐦 Bird Watching

Look and listen for birds near trees. Talk about what birds need and where they may rest or find food.

🏡 Animal Homes

Investigate nests, holes, branches, and other places animals may use for shelter.

🐞 Insect Investigations

Observe insects safely and discuss how they move, where they live, and how they interact with plants and trees.

🌰 Pinecone Exploration

Compare pinecones by size, shape, texture, and color. Invite children to sort and describe their discoveries.

🎨 Nature Art

Create artwork inspired by leaves, sticks, bark, pinecones, and other natural materials.

Questions to Ask

Who lives in trees?
What do animals use trees for?
How do birds build nests?
What insects can we find near trees?
How can we observe living things safely?
