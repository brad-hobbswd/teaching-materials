Trees Study

Week 4: Why Trees Matter

Children discover how trees help people, animals, communities, and our planet.

Back to Trees StudyExplore Activities

Weekly Investigations

Help children connect their study of trees to community life, everyday materials, environmental care, and responsible choices.

🌳 Community Trees

Look for trees around the school or neighborhood and discuss how they provide shade, beauty, and places for living things.

🪵 Wood Products

Explore familiar objects made from wood and discuss how trees become useful materials.

🌎 Protecting Nature

Talk about ways children and adults can care for trees and natural spaces.

♻️ Recycling Paper

Sort paper for reuse or recycling and connect paper products to trees and responsible resource use.

🎉 Celebration Project

Create a class tree project that shows what children learned and why trees matter.

Questions to Ask

How do trees help people?
How do trees help animals?
What products come from trees?
How can we care for trees?
Why is recycling paper helpful?
