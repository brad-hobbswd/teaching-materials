Nature and Science Study

🐞 Insects

Invite children to investigate insects through observation, movement, art, literacy, mathematics, sensory exploration, outdoor discovery, and thoughtful questions. Children can notice how insects move, where they live, what they need, and how they contribute to the world around us.

Four Week Study Interest Areas Essential Questions

Four Week Investigation

Week 1

Meet the Insects

Begin with close observation and children's existing ideas about insects.

  • Insect walks
  • Observation drawings
  • Body part vocabulary
  • Magnifying glass exploration
Week 2

How Insects Move

Compare crawling, flying, jumping, and other forms of insect movement.

  • Movement games
  • Counting legs
  • Wing observations
  • Movement patterns
Week 3

Insects and Habitats

Investigate where insects live and what they need to survive.

  • Habitat hunts
  • Soil exploration
  • Garden observations
  • Habitat building
Week 4

Insects in Our World

Explore pollination, food webs, gardens, decomposition, and the role insects play in ecosystems.

  • Pollinator study
  • Garden connections
  • Nature art
  • Family insect hunt

Essential Questions

What makes an insect an insect?

Compare insect body parts and notice similarities and differences.

How do insects move?

Observe and represent different types of movement.

Where do insects live?

Search for habitats and discuss what insects need.

How do insects help the environment?

Explore pollination, decomposition, and food chains in age appropriate ways.

Interest Area Ideas

🔬 Discovery

Provide magnifiers, insect photographs, natural materials, and observation journals.

🎨 Art

Create symmetrical butterflies, insect collages, prints, and observational drawings.

📚 Library

Add nonfiction books, insect stories, field guides, and vocabulary cards.

🧱 Blocks

Build gardens, insect habitats, tunnels, nests, and bridges.

🎭 Dramatic Play

Create a garden center, nature research station, or insect explorer camp.

🧮 Math

Sort insect pictures, count legs, compare sizes, create patterns, and graph observations.

💧 Sensory

Use soil, leaves, sticks, stones, and safe insect figures for habitat exploration.

🌳 Outdoors

Look beneath leaves, near garden plants, and around natural spaces while practicing respectful observation.
