💪 Week 1: Discovering Movement

Children explore how their bodies move through walking, running, jumping, stretching, and active play.

Back to Exercise StudyNext Week →
WEEK 1

How Can Our Bodies Move?

Invite children to notice different movements and describe what their bodies can do.

🏃

Movement Hunt

Walk, march, tiptoe, hop, and move in different ways around the classroom.

🤸

Stretch & Reach

Explore gentle stretches and compare high, low, wide, and small body positions.

🗣️

Movement Words

Build vocabulary with words such as move, stretch, jump, balance, fast, slow, high, and low.
