Investigate & Care

Connect recycling to community life through observation, conversation, cleanup, and problem solving.

Learning Experiences

  • Observe waste and recycling systems in the community.
  • Discuss litter and how it affects living things.
  • Plan a classroom or playground cleanup.
  • Create signs that encourage responsible waste habits.
