Our Commitment

Accessibility Statement

Little Explorers Learning Hub aims to make its educational information and classroom resources usable by as many educators and families as possible.

Accessibility practices

  • Use descriptive page titles, headings, and link text where practical.
  • Provide alternative text for meaningful images when available.
  • Use responsive layouts intended to work across desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
  • Consider readability, keyboard access, contrast, and clear page structure when updating the site.

Documents and downloads

Some downloadable documents or older pages may not yet meet every accessibility need. If a file is difficult to use with assistive technology, contact us and describe the page or file. We will consider an accessible alternative or correction.

Feedback and assistance

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please use the Contact page. Include the page URL, the problem you encountered, and any assistive technology or format preference you would like us to consider. Please do not include confidential information about children or families.

We will use feedback to prioritize improvements to the site.
