🔬 Science & Discovery

Encourage children to observe closely, ask questions, make predictions, investigate materials, notice changes, and share what they discover.

Questions to Explore

What do you notice? What do you think will happen? What changed? How could we find out?

Investigation Ideas

Explore water, light and shadows, magnets, ramps, sound, textures, plants, weather, and changing materials.

Teacher Documentation

Record children's questions, predictions, actions, discoveries, explanations, and new questions.
