💻 Technology & Tools

Use developmentally appropriate tools and technology to help children communicate, investigate, create, document, and solve problems.

Tool Exploration

Explore safe classroom tools such as magnifiers, measuring tools, flashlights, cameras, rulers, and building materials.

Digital Creation

Use appropriate digital tools for photography, audio, drawing, documentation, research with adult support, and creative expression.

Purposeful Use

Choose tools because they help children investigate, communicate, create, or solve a meaningful problem.
