A practical planning cycle

Curriculum planning is an ongoing cycle. Follow children's interests, connect experiences to developmental goals, observe what happens, and use what you learn to plan what comes next.

  1. Notice: Observe children's play, questions, conversations, and repeated interests.
  2. Choose: Select a study or investigation that can build on those interests.
  3. Plan: Prepare open-ended materials, intentional interactions, and flexible experiences.
  4. Observe: Record what children say, do, try, and revisit.
  5. Reflect and adapt: Adjust materials, support, pace, and next steps for individual children.
  6. Connect with families: Invite family knowledge, home languages, ideas, and experiences into the learning.
