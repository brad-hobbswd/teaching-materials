PROFESSIONAL REFLECTION

Pause. Examine. Learn. Grow.

Reflection turns experience into professional learning. Use a simple cycle to examine what happened, what you noticed, what you learned, and what you want to try next.

🔎

What Happened?

Describe the experience without rushing to judgment. Identify the context, decisions, interactions, and observations that matter.

  • What did I notice?
  • What did children do?
  • What did I do?
💭

What Did I Learn?

Consider what the experience reveals about your practice, children's needs, the environment, or your assumptions.

  • What worked?
  • What challenged me?
  • What surprised me?
➡️

What Comes Next?

Turn reflection into a practical next step that can be tried, observed, and revisited.

  • What will I change?
  • What will I keep?
  • What will I investigate?

Reflection Tool

Learn: What did I learn?   Reflect: What does it mean?   Apply: What will I try?   Grow: What will I look for next?
