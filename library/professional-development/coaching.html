COACHING & MENTORING

Grow Through Conversation and Reflection

Strong coaching creates space to examine practice, ask useful questions, consider evidence, and turn reflection into meaningful next steps.

👂

Listen First

Begin with curiosity. Understand the educator's experience before moving toward solutions.

  • Ask open questions
  • Listen for strengths and concerns
  • Clarify the goal
💬

Give Useful Feedback

Feedback should be specific, respectful, connected to practice, and focused on growth.

  • Describe observable practice
  • Identify strengths
  • Offer a practical next step
🤝

Build Collaboration

Mentoring becomes stronger when educators learn with one another and share professional knowledge.

  • Set shared goals
  • Model strategies
  • Follow up on progress

Coaching Prompt

What evidence from the classroom would help you understand the situation more clearly before deciding what the next step should be?
