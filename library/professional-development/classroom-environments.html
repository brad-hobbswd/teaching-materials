CLASSROOM ENVIRONMENTS

Create Spaces That Support Learning

The classroom environment is part of the teaching process. Thoughtful spaces can support children's participation, independence, relationships, exploration, and learning.

🛋️

Design With Purpose

Consider how the arrangement of materials, pathways, and learning areas supports children's experiences.

  • Make materials accessible
  • Create clear pathways
  • Support different kinds of play
🧸

Support Independence

Arrange materials and routines so children can participate meaningfully and make appropriate choices.

  • Use child accessible materials
  • Offer clear choices
  • Encourage responsibility
🔎

Reflect and Adjust

Observe how children use the environment and make changes based on what you notice.

  • Watch where children gather
  • Notice barriers
  • Adjust and observe again

Environment Reflection

What does your classroom environment make easy for children to do, and what might be made easier with a thoughtful change?
