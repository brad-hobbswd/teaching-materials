CDA SUPPORT

Build a Strong Professional Portfolio

Use organized planning, documentation, reflection, and professional learning to support your CDA journey and strengthen your practice.

🗂️

Plan Your Evidence

Organize classroom examples, professional experiences, and documentation that demonstrate your developing practice.

  • Identify evidence you already have
  • Record examples as they occur
  • Keep materials organized
📝

Document Your Practice

Use clear documentation to connect everyday teaching experiences with professional learning and reflection.

  • Describe what happened
  • Connect evidence to practice
  • Reflect on what you learned
🌱

Keep Growing

Treat credential work as part of continued professional growth rather than a single training requirement.

  • Set professional goals
  • Seek feedback
  • Apply new learning

Reflection Prompt

What part of your current teaching practice best demonstrates the educator you are becoming, and what evidence could help you explain that growth?
