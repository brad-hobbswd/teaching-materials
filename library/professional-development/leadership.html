LEADERSHIP DEVELOPMENT

Lead With Purpose and Integrity

Leadership in early childhood grows through everyday decisions, professional communication, collaboration, problem solving, and a commitment to children, families, and colleagues.

🧭

Lead Yourself

Build self awareness, reliability, professionalism, and a commitment to continued learning.

  • Set clear professional goals
  • Reflect on decisions
  • Follow through consistently
👥

Lead With Others

Strengthen teams through listening, communication, collaboration, shared responsibility, and respectful problem solving.

  • Communicate clearly
  • Invite different perspectives
  • Support shared goals
💡

Lead Change

Use evidence, reflection, and thoughtful planning when improving classroom or program practices.

  • Identify the need
  • Consider evidence
  • Plan and evaluate change

Leadership Reflection

How do your everyday actions influence the children, families, colleagues, and professional community around you?
