CONTINUING EDUCATION

Make Professional Learning Ongoing

Professional development is strongest when learning continues beyond a single training. Use a purposeful cycle of learning, application, reflection, and continued growth.

📖

Choose Learning Goals

Identify areas of practice where new knowledge, skills, or perspectives could strengthen your work.

  • Identify a growth area
  • Set a practical goal
  • Choose relevant learning
🛠️

Apply What You Learn

Move professional learning into classroom practice so new ideas can be observed and considered.

  • Try a strategy
  • Observe what happens
  • Adjust as needed
🌱

Reflect and Continue

Use reflection to decide what to keep, change, investigate, or learn next.

  • Record key learning
  • Reflect on impact
  • Set the next goal

Learning Prompt

What professional learning would make the greatest difference in your classroom right now, and how will you know whether it helped?
