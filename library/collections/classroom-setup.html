🌟 A Classroom Designed for Learning

More Than Just Arranging Furniture

A thoughtful classroom setup helps children know where to go, what to expect, and how to participate in their learning environment. The arrangement of spaces, materials, furniture, and interest areas can support independence, exploration, relationships, and smooth daily routines.

Use these resources to think intentionally about how your classroom works from the moment children arrive until the end of the day.

💡 Quick Tips

Before Children Arrive

01

Make important materials easy for children to see and reach.

02

Keep pathways clear and consider how children move throughout the classroom.

03

Create spaces that encourage both independent exploration and social interaction.

04

Revisit your classroom arrangement as children's needs and interests change.
