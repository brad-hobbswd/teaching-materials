🔄 Smooth Transitions

Make Moving From One Activity to Another Easier

Transitions are a regular part of the classroom day. Consistent cues and predictable steps can help children understand what is happening and prepare for what comes next.

01

Give a Cue

Let children know when an activity is ending and what will happen next.

02

Keep It Predictable

Use familiar words, signals, songs, or visual supports whenever possible.

03

Allow Time

Give children enough time to finish, clean up, and move to the next experience.

04

Practice the Routine

Teach routines intentionally instead of assuming children already know what to do.
