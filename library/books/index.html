TEACHER PLANNING SUPPORT

Build Literacy Into Everyday Learning

Literacy development can happen throughout the day. Use these planning areas to intentionally incorporate language, books, conversation, writing, and storytelling.

01

Talk & Listen

Create meaningful conversations that encourage children to describe, explain, question, listen, and share ideas.

02

Read Together

Provide engaging books and interactive read alouds that invite children to make connections and discuss stories.

03

Write & Create

Give children opportunities to draw, scribble, write, label, dictate stories, and communicate through print.

04

Tell & Retell

Encourage storytelling, dramatic play, sequencing, retelling, and children's own creative narratives.

INTENTIONAL LITERACY PRACTICE

Listen. Read. Talk. Respond.

Strong literacy environments begin with noticing what children are interested in and creating opportunities for them to communicate. Observe children's language, questions, stories, drawings, and writing, then use what you learn to plan meaningful next steps.
