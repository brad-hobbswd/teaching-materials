✏️ Writing & Mark Making

Support children's early writing by providing materials, purpose, time, and encouragement to communicate through marks and print.

EMERGENT WRITING

Give Children Reasons to Write

Writing develops through exploration. Offer varied materials and meaningful reasons for children to draw, mark, copy, label, sign, and write.

🖍 Mark Making

Provide crayons, chalk, paint, markers, pencils, and sensory materials for purposeful exploration.

✍️ Name Writing

Use children's names in authentic classroom experiences without turning writing into a worksheet only activity.

📝 Purposeful Print

Invite children to make signs, lists, menus, messages, labels, plans, and notes during play.

🤲 Fine Motor Support

Strengthen hand and finger control through varied drawing, building, manipulating, and sensory experiences.
