🎭 Storytelling & Retelling

Build narrative language by giving children opportunities to tell, retell, act out, sequence, and invent stories.

NARRATIVE LANGUAGE

Let Children Become Storytellers

Stories can grow from books, real experiences, dramatic play, children's drawings, photographs, classroom studies, and imagination.

🎭 Dramatic Play

Provide props and roles that encourage children to use language, negotiate ideas, and act out experiences.

🧸 Story Props

Use puppets, felt pieces, objects, photographs, and simple props to support retelling.

🔢 Sequencing

Talk about what happened first, next, and last while encouraging children to connect events.

💭 Story Creation

Invite children to invent characters, settings, problems, solutions, and endings.
