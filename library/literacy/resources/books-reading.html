📖 Books & Reading

Use shared reading and everyday book experiences to build comprehension, print awareness, vocabulary, and a love of stories.

BOOK EXPERIENCES

Make Reading Interactive

Children learn about language and literacy when books are part of relationships, conversations, play, and meaningful classroom experiences.

📚 Shared Reading

Pause, wonder, predict, describe illustrations, and invite children to participate in repeated readings.

🔎 Print Awareness

Notice book parts, print direction, letters, words, signs, labels, and environmental print.

🧠 Story Comprehension

Talk about characters, settings, events, problems, solutions, feelings, and children's connections.

❤️ Book Choice

Provide accessible books that reflect children's interests, families, cultures, experiences, and curiosity.
