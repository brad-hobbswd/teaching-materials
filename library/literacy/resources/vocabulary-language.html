💬 Vocabulary & Language

Support children's communication through rich conversations, responsive interactions, and meaningful vocabulary experiences.

LANGUAGE DEVELOPMENT

Make Conversation Part of Learning

Children build language when adults listen, respond, model language, introduce new words, and invite children to share their thinking.

💬 Rich Conversations

Ask open ended questions and give children time to explain, describe, predict, and respond.

🧠 New Vocabulary

Introduce precise words during play, routines, studies, books, and real experiences.

👂 Listening & Responding

Model attentive listening and expand children's ideas without taking over the conversation.

🏡 Family Language

Honor home languages and invite families to share words, stories, songs, and communication traditions.
