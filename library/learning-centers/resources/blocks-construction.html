🧱 Blocks & Construction

Use blocks and loose parts to support spatial reasoning, balance, measurement, design, collaboration, and problem solving.

Build, Test, Revise

Offer varied blocks, connectors, recycled materials, ramps, signs, rulers, clipboards, and photographs of structures. Invite children to plan, build, explain, test, and revise their ideas.
