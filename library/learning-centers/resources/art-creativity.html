🎨 Art & Creativity

Provide open ended materials that encourage experimentation, choice, representation, communication, and creative expression.

Process Over Product

Offer paper, paint, clay, collage materials, drawing tools, natural materials, and recycled items. Ask children about their ideas without requiring a specific finished product.
