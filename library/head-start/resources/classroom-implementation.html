CLASSROOM PRACTICE

Turn Frameworks Into Experiences

Use classroom routines, environments, interactions, and learning experiences as opportunities to support children's development.

🏫 Environment

Arrange materials, spaces, and learning centers so children can explore, communicate, make choices, and practice emerging skills.

🧩 Experiences

Plan developmentally appropriate experiences that respond to children's interests and developmental needs.

💬 Interactions

Use responsive interactions, conversations, modeling, questioning, and encouragement to extend children's learning.

🔄 Routines

Use everyday routines and transitions as meaningful opportunities for learning, independence, relationships, and communication.
