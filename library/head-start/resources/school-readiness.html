PLANNING SUPPORT

Make Goals Visible in Daily Practice

Use school readiness goals to help organize intentional experiences and reflect on children's progress over time.

🎯 Identify

Identify the readiness goal or developmental focus connected to the children and learning experience.

👀 Observe

Gather authentic evidence during play, routines, conversations, exploration, and group experiences.

📝 Track

Organize observations and work samples so progress can be reviewed over time.

🤝 Partner

Include family knowledge and perspectives when considering children's strengths, interests, and next steps.
