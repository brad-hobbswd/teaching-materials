Connect Developmental Goals to Evidence

Use domain and goal information as a starting point for observing children and planning responsive learning experiences.

📚 Know the Domain

Identify the developmental area and goal that best connects to the learning you are observing.

👀 Observe

Look for authentic evidence of children's skills, communication, exploration, and interactions.

📝 Document

Capture meaningful evidence using objective notes, work samples, photographs, and learning stories.

🎯 Plan

Use what you learned to design intentional experiences that support children's continued development.
