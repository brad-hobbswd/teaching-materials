DOCUMENTATION PRACTICE

Observe With Purpose

Strong documentation begins with noticing authentic moments and recording evidence that can inform understanding, reflection, and planning.

👀 Notice

Watch for children's interests, communication, relationships, problem solving, exploration, and emerging skills.

📝 Record

Use objective language to capture what happened, what the child said or did, and the context of the experience.

🔎 Interpret

Consider what the evidence may show about children's development, learning, strengths, and interests.

🎯 Respond

Use the information to plan intentional experiences, interactions, materials, and learning extensions.
