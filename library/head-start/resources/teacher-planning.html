PLANNING CYCLE

Plan From What You Know About Children

Use a simple cycle of noticing, reflecting, planning, implementing, and revisiting to make classroom experiences responsive and intentional.

👀 Start With Observation

Identify children's interests, strengths, questions, skills, relationships, and emerging needs.

🎯 Choose a Focus

Connect the observation to developmental goals, learning objectives, or school readiness priorities.

🧩 Plan the Experience

Select materials, environment, interactions, questions, and experiences that can extend learning.

🔄 Revisit

Observe children's responses and use the evidence to adjust future experiences and next steps.
