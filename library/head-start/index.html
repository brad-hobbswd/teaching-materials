School Readiness: From Frameworks to Classroom Practice

School readiness is the progress children make across developmental domains as they build the skills, confidence, relationships, and knowledge that support future learning. Use these resources to connect program expectations with what you observe and do with children every day.

How School Readiness Works in Practice

Use this cycle to connect expectations with real evidence of children’s development and make informed decisions about next steps.

01

Understand

Review the relevant Head Start standards, ELOF domains, and developmental goals.

02

Observe

Watch for children's interests, skills, behaviors, questions, and developmental progress.

03

Document

Record meaningful evidence of children's learning through observations and work samples.

04

Plan

Use what you learn about children to plan intentional experiences and learning extensions.

INTENTIONAL TEACHING

Use What You Learn to Support Each Child

School readiness grows through responsive teaching. Observe children in everyday experiences, document meaningful evidence, partner with families, and adjust support to reflect each child’s strengths, interests, and needs.
