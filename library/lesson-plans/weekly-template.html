Weekly Lesson Plan Template

A practical, editable planning sheet for Early Head Start, Head Start, preschool, and Pre-K. Connect children's interests with intentional teaching, developmental goals, inclusive supports, observations, and family partnerships.

Plan for meaningful learning, then adjust from what you observe

Complete the fields online, then choose Print or Save as PDF. This page does not save entries after you leave or refresh it, so download or print a copy for your records.

Alignment reminder: Use the Head Start Early Learning Outcomes Framework (ELOF) to guide planning and observation. Select goals that fit the children's age, developmental progression, individual strengths, and current learning. This is an independent planning resource, not an official Head Start form or a replacement for your program's approved curriculum and assessment procedures.
