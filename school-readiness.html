From Knowledge to Practice

A Practical School Readiness Cycle

Use this process to connect developmental expectations with real experiences and decisions that support each child.

01

Understand

Review applicable frameworks, developmental domains, program goals, and expectations.

02

Observe

Notice children's interests, strengths, emerging skills, interactions, and questions.

03

Document

Record meaningful evidence through observations, conversations, and work samples.

04

Respond

Plan appropriate experiences, collaborate with families, and identify helpful next steps.

Educator reminder: Use observations and assessment information as part of an ongoing process. Interpret evidence in context, consider each child's strengths and needs, and follow your program's applicable assessment and documentation procedures.
