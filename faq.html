Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about using Little Explorers Learning Hub and its free early childhood education resources.

Are the resources free?

Yes. The site is designed to provide educators with free planning ideas, classroom tools, printable resources, and professional support. Review the terms on any third party resource before reusing it.

Which age groups are included?

Resources are organized for Early Head Start, toddlers, preschool, and Pre Kindergarten. Choose an age group and adapt every experience to the children’s individual development, interests, language, culture, and support needs.

How do resources connect to Head Start?

School readiness resources are intended to help educators plan experiences connected to the Head Start Early Learning Outcomes Framework (ELOF). Use your program’s current standards, assessment process, curriculum guidance, and local policies when planning.

Is this an official Creative Curriculum website?

No. Little Explorers Learning Hub is an independent resource site. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Teaching Strategies. Resources may support broad, play-based planning practices but do not replace licensed curriculum materials.

Can I use the resources with children who need extra behavioral or developmental support?

Use positive, relationship-based guidance and adapt activities to individual needs. Documentation on this site is for educational planning, not diagnosis. Partner with families, program specialists, and qualified service providers when additional support is needed.

How do I report a broken link or suggest a resource?

Please use the Contact page and include the page address, what you expected to find, and the resource you would like to see added.
