WELLNESS IS PART OF THE WORK

You cannot pour from an empty classroom.

Teaching is meaningful work, but meaningful work can also require tremendous energy. Teachers give attention, patience, creativity, encouragement, organization, and emotional presence throughout the day.

Professional wellness is not about creating a perfect workday. It is about developing sustainable practices that help educators remain organized, encouraged, reflective, and able to continue doing meaningful work.

Wellness looks different for every educator. The goal is to identify practical supports that make teaching more sustainable.

SIX FOUNDATIONS

Build Wellness Into the Workday

Wellness does not have to be complicated. Small, intentional practices can help teachers create more manageable and sustainable days.

01
⚖️
BALANCE

Protect Your Energy

Recognize that your energy is a resource. Consider where your attention goes during the day and identify places where routines, boundaries, or delegation can reduce unnecessary strain.

Consider: What is taking energy that could be redirected?
02
📋
ORGANIZATION

Create Systems That Help You

Simple systems for planning, materials, documentation, communication, and daily routines can reduce decision fatigue and create more space for teaching.

Consider: What repeated task needs a better system?
03
🌿
PAUSE

Make Room to Reset

Brief moments of intentional pause can help you reset your attention and return to the classroom with greater clarity and presence.

Consider: Where can I create a genuine moment to pause?
04
💛
ENCOURAGEMENT

Notice What Is Going Well

Teaching can make challenges highly visible. Intentionally noticing progress, relationships, meaningful moments, and small victories can help restore perspective.

Consider: What went well today that deserves notice?
05
🤝
CONNECTION

Do Not Teach Alone

Healthy professional relationships can provide encouragement, collaboration, perspective, problem solving, and a sense of shared purpose.

Consider: Who can I learn from or encourage this week?
06
🌱
REFLECTION

Keep Growing Without Losing Yourself

Professional growth should strengthen your practice without requiring you to become someone else. Reflect on what works, what needs adjustment, and what you want to learn.

Consider: What is one change that would support me?
THE DAILY RESET

A better day does not always require a bigger plan.

Sometimes the most helpful wellness practice is simply creating a small transition between one part of the day and the next.

A few intentional moments can help you close one experience, reset your attention, and prepare for what comes next.

60 SECOND RESET Pause before you continue.
01
Breathe

Take one slow breath and release the tension you are carrying.

02
Notice

Identify what you are feeling, thinking, or needing.

03
Choose

Decide what deserves your attention next rather than reacting automatically.

04
Continue

Move forward with intention and give yourself permission to begin again.

ORGANIZATION SUPPORTS WELLNESS

When systems carry some of the load, teachers can focus on teaching.

Organization is not about making every minute perfectly scheduled. It is about creating predictable systems that reduce unnecessary mental work.

🗓️

Plan

Identify the most important priorities before the day becomes busy.

📦

Simplify

Keep frequently used materials and systems easy to access and easy to maintain.

📝

Document

Use consistent systems for observations, notes, communication, and planning.

🔄

Review

Regularly ask which systems are helping and which ones need to change.

A WORD FOR THE EDUCATOR

You do not have to do everything perfectly.

There will be difficult days. There will be lessons that do not go according to plan, paperwork that feels overwhelming, behaviors that challenge you, and moments when you wonder whether you are making a difference.

Those moments do not erase the meaningful work you are doing.

Give yourself room to learn, adjust, ask for help, celebrate progress, and begin again. Professional growth is a process, and sustainable teaching requires grace for the educator as well as the children.

REMEMBER Progress is still progress, even when the day was difficult.
✦
TEACHER REFLECTION

What do you need to sustain your work?

Take a few minutes to consider your current teaching experience. Wellness begins with noticing. You do not have to solve everything today. Start by identifying one area where a small change could make your work more sustainable.

Protect What boundary would help protect my energy?
Simplify What could I make easier through a better system?
Restore What helps me feel encouraged and renewed?
