INTENTIONAL TEACHING

Teaching Strategies That Extend Learning

ELOF aligned teaching does not require turning every classroom experience into a formal lesson. Meaningful learning often happens through play, routines, conversations, relationships, exploration, and responsive interactions.

Use these strategies throughout the day to notice learning and intentionally extend it.

01

Ask Open Ended Questions

Invite children to explain their thinking, make predictions, describe experiences, and consider possibilities.

02

Model New Skills

Demonstrate language, problem solving, social interaction, movement, literacy, and other skills within meaningful classroom experiences.

03

Scaffold Learning

Provide just enough support to help children move toward greater independence.

04

Follow Children's Interests

Use children's questions, interests, discoveries, and play as opportunities for deeper learning.

05

Extend the Experience

Add materials, vocabulary, questions, challenges, or new perspectives that move learning forward.

06

Reflect and Plan

Use observations to determine what children may be ready to explore, practice, or experience next.

OBSERVATION

Look for Learning in the Moment

A child's learning may be visible in a conversation, a block structure, a drawing, a movement experience, a conflict with a peer, a science discovery, or an everyday routine.

Instead of asking only whether an activity was completed, ask what the child demonstrated during the experience.
