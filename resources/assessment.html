Why Observation Matters

Observation is one of the most valuable teaching practices in early childhood education. By carefully watching children during play, conversations, routines, and exploration, teachers gain meaningful insights into development, interests, relationships, and learning. Authentic observations help educators plan engaging experiences, individualize instruction, celebrate growth, and strengthen family partnerships.

Best Practice

Authentic Assessment in Early Childhood

Authentic assessment happens naturally throughout the day while children play, communicate, investigate, create, and interact with others. Rather than relying only on formal assessments, teachers gather meaningful evidence during everyday experiences to understand each child's strengths, interests, and developmental progress.
