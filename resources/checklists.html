STAY ORGANIZED

Small Systems Make a Big Difference

Checklists are most useful when they become part of a predictable classroom routine rather than another task to remember.

01

Keep It Visible

Place frequently used checklists where they are easy to see and use during the routine they support.

02

Make It Practical

A useful checklist should help you remember important responsibilities without creating unnecessary paperwork.

03

Review Regularly

Update checklists when classroom routines, responsibilities, or program needs change.

04

Reflect and Improve

Use completed checklists to identify patterns, missed responsibilities, and opportunities to improve classroom systems.
