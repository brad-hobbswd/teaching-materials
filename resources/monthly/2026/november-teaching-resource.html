November 2026 Teaching Resource

Gratitude, Families, Community, and Seasonal Discovery

Purpose: Use these ideas flexibly. Focus on children's relationships, communication, play, inquiry, and family experiences. Seasonal or holiday references should be adapted to the cultures and traditions represented in each classroom.

Week 1: Changes in Our Community

  • Science: Observe cooler weather, changing trees, daylight, and outdoor materials.
  • Language: Introduce words such as change, prepare, warm, cool, gather, and notice.
  • Math: Count and compare classroom collections and natural materials.
  • Art: Create collaborative seasonal collages using safe natural materials.

Week 2: Families and Belonging

  • Language: Invite children to share names, routines, people, foods, songs, and traditions that are meaningful to them.
  • Social emotional: Explore belonging, helping, caring, and feeling safe with trusted adults.
  • Books: Select stories representing the children and families in the classroom.
  • Dramatic play: Provide open-ended household and community props for family role play.

Week 3: Gratitude and Helping

  • Social emotional: Model simple ways children can notice and express appreciation.
  • Language: Create a class gratitude conversation using children's own words.
  • Math: Count classroom acts of helping or sort picture cards showing ways people help.
  • Art: Make a collaborative “people who help us” display.

Week 4: Cooking, Sharing, and Seasonal Discovery

  • Science: Explore changes in ingredients through safe food preparation experiences.
  • Math: Measure, compare, count, and sequence simple preparation steps.
  • Language: Describe smells, textures, tastes, and changes using rich vocabulary.
  • Social emotional: Practice sharing, cooperation, turn taking, and caring for classroom materials.

Family Connection

Invite families to share something meaningful about their home life, such as a favorite routine, song, story, food, or way they spend time together. Families may share only what feels comfortable.

Materials

Family photos when available, books, natural materials, baskets, sorting objects, dramatic play props, paper, washable art materials, measuring tools, and safe food ingredients when permitted.

Teacher Observation

Notice children's communication, relationships, participation in group experiences, classification and counting skills, pretend play, and ability to express preferences or appreciation.

Adaptation

Honor family differences and avoid assuming that all children celebrate the same holidays or traditions. Provide visual supports, alternative materials, multiple communication methods, and meaningful choices.
