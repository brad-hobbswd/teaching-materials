FAMILY PARTNERSHIPS

Engagement Is More Than Communication

Meaningful family engagement is an ongoing process of building respectful, strengths based relationships between educators and families. Families bring valuable knowledge about their children, and educators bring professional knowledge and classroom experience. When those perspectives come together, children benefit.

🤝

Partner

Work with families rather than simply doing things for families.

👂

Listen

Make space for family knowledge, priorities, questions, experiences, and goals.

💬

Communicate

Keep communication clear, respectful, consistent, and two way.

🌱

Grow Together

Build shared goals that support children, families, classrooms, and communities.

TWO WAY COMMUNICATION

Make Communication a Conversation

Communication should not move in only one direction. Families should have meaningful opportunities to share what they know, ask questions, express concerns, celebrate accomplishments, and contribute to classroom decision making.

A message home can be more than a reminder. It can open a door.

01

Share

Tell families what children are learning and experiencing.

02

Ask

Invite families to share knowledge, interests, questions, and ideas.

03

Listen

Give family perspectives genuine consideration.

04

Respond

Follow through so families know their voice matters.

LEARNING AT HOME

Simple Ideas Families Can Use Anywhere

Family activities do not need expensive materials or complicated instructions. Everyday routines can become meaningful learning opportunities.

📖

Read Together

Talk about pictures, characters, events, feelings, and predictions while reading.

Language • Literacy
🥣

Cook Together

Count ingredients, compare amounts, describe textures, follow directions, and talk together.

Math • Science • Language
🚶

Explore Outside

Look for patterns, observe nature, collect observations, move, compare, and ask questions.

Science • Cognition • Physical
🧺

Sort and Organize

Sort laundry, toys, utensils, or other safe household objects by color, size, shape, or use.

Mathematics • Cognition
🎵

Sing Together

Use songs, rhymes, rhythm, movement, and repetition to create joyful learning.

Language • Literacy • Physical
💭

Ask One More Question

When children make a statement, respond with curiosity and invite them to explain their thinking.

Language • Cognition
BUILDING TRUST

Families Are Experts on Their Children

Families hold knowledge, experiences, traditions, strengths, goals, and insights that educators cannot get from observation alone.

Strong partnerships make room for that expertise. Ask families what they notice at home. Ask what their child enjoys. Ask what works. Ask what they want their child to learn.

?

What does your child enjoy doing at home?

?

What strengths do you see in your child?

?

What would you like us to work on together?

?

What would help your family feel supported?

01

→

04
