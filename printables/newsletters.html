WHAT TO INCLUDE

Give Families a Window Into the Classroom

A monthly newsletter can become a simple, consistent bridge between classroom learning and family life.

01

What We Are Learning

Share current classroom topics, children's interests, projects, investigations, books, or learning experiences.

02

Important Dates

Include upcoming classroom events, conferences, celebrations, meetings, closures, and reminders.

03

Family Connection

Invite families to share experiences, participate in classroom activities, or continue learning at home.

04

Helpful Reminders

Provide practical reminders about clothing, supplies, attendance, communication, classroom routines, or upcoming needs.

MONTHLY COMMUNICATION

Make Every Newsletter Meaningful

Families do not need a long newsletter. They need useful information that helps them understand what is happening in their child's classroom and how they can connect.

Keep the language welcoming, clear, and easy to read. Highlight the experiences that matter most and give families practical ways to participate.

✓
Keep information clear

Use short sections and familiar language families can understand.

✓
Celebrate learning

Share meaningful classroom experiences and children's interests.

✓
Invite partnership

Give families simple opportunities to connect with classroom learning.

✓
Be consistent

A predictable monthly communication routine helps families know what to expect.
