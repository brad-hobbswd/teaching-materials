CLASSROOM PLANNING

Make Every Center Intentional

A strong learning center is more than a collection of materials. Thoughtful planning helps teachers create invitations that encourage children to explore, communicate, solve problems, collaborate, and extend their learning.

Use center planning to consider the materials, questions, vocabulary, developmental goals, observation opportunities, and ways children can independently engage with the environment.

Explore Lesson Planning

Center Planning Checklist

✓

Is the center inviting and accessible?

✓

Are materials organized and clearly presented?

✓

Can children make meaningful choices?

✓

Are there opportunities for language and interaction?

✓

Does the center encourage exploration and problem solving?

✓

Can teachers observe and document learning?

LEARNING ENVIRONMENTS

Centers That Invite Children to Learn

Thoughtfully designed learning environments give children room to make choices, follow interests, develop skills, and learn through active participation.

01

Invite

Arrange materials in ways that spark curiosity and communicate what children can explore.

02

Explore

Give children time and space to investigate materials, ideas, relationships, and possibilities.

03

Extend

Use questions, new materials, vocabulary, and challenges to deepen children's thinking.

04

Observe

Watch what children do, say, create, and discover to guide future planning and instruction.
