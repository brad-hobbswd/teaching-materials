Frequently Asked Questions

Answers To Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our alphabet and literacy printable resources.

What ages are these resources designed for?

Our printables are created for Early Head Start, Head Start, Preschool, and Pre Kindergarten learners.

Can I use these in literacy centers?

Yes. Every resource is designed to work well in independent centers, small groups, and whole group instruction.

Are the printables classroom ready?

Absolutely. Simply print, laminate if desired, and they're ready to use.

Are new resources added regularly?

Yes. New alphabet activities and literacy printables are added throughout the year.
