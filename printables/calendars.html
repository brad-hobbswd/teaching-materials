CALENDAR LEARNING

Turn Calendar Time Into Meaningful Learning

Calendar materials can support much more than naming the date. Use them to encourage observation, language, mathematics, science, and classroom participation.

🔢

Mathematics

Explore counting, number recognition, sequencing, patterns, comparison, and simple data collection.

💬

Language

Encourage children to talk about the day, describe weather, ask questions, and use new vocabulary.

🔬

Science

Use weather and seasonal observations to help children notice changes and patterns in their environment.

🤝

Community

Give children opportunities to participate in familiar classroom routines and take meaningful classroom roles.

CLASSROOM IDEAS

Make the Calendar Part of the Classroom

Calendar resources can be displayed on a dedicated calendar board, incorporated into morning meeting, or used throughout the classroom as children explore time and change.

The goal is not simply to memorize names and dates. The calendar can become a daily opportunity for children to observe, talk, count, compare, predict, and participate.

01
Begin With Today

Invite children to identify the current day and date.

02
Observe the Weather

Ask children what they notice outside and record the observation.

03
Notice Patterns

Compare today's information with previous days and look for patterns.

04
Give Children a Role

Let children help move cards, record weather, or update the date.
