MORE THAN A JOB CHART

Responsibility Is Part of the Learning

Classroom helper roles can become meaningful opportunities for children to practice skills that extend beyond the job itself.

🧠

Decision Making

Children practice making choices and understanding what needs to happen next.

💪

Independence

Repeated classroom responsibilities provide opportunities to practice completing familiar tasks with increasing independence.

💬

Communication

Children communicate with classmates and teachers while carrying out classroom roles.

❤️

Belonging

Helping the classroom can strengthen children's sense that they are valued members of their learning community.

CLASSROOM IMPLEMENTATION

Make Classroom Jobs Meaningful

A job chart works best when classroom jobs are simple, purposeful, and connected to children's actual classroom experiences.

Introduce one role at a time, model what the responsibility looks like, and give children opportunities to practice. Jobs can rotate regularly so children experience different ways of contributing.

01
Explain the Job

Show children what the responsibility looks like.

02
Model the Routine

Demonstrate the steps before children take responsibility for the job.

03
Rotate Roles

Give children opportunities to experience different responsibilities.

04
Celebrate Helping

Notice children's contributions and reinforce their sense of belonging.
