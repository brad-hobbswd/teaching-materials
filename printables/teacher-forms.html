EVERYDAY DOCUMENTATION

Keep Forms Simple and Useful

Teacher forms should make documentation easier, not create another complicated task. Choose forms that fit naturally into your classroom routines and record information that has a clear purpose.

Keep records factual, organized, and easy to revisit so they can support planning, reflection, communication, and classroom decision making.

01
Record What Matters

Focus on information that supports teaching and children's experiences.

02
Be Objective

Record observable information rather than assumptions or conclusions.

03
Keep Records Organized

Use consistent systems so information can be found when it is needed.

04
Connect Documentation to Practice

Use what you document to inform planning, reflection, and communication.
