INTENTIONAL ORGANIZATION

Labels Can Support Independence

Classroom labels are more than decoration. When used intentionally, visual labels can help children understand classroom routines, locate materials, participate in cleanup, and develop independence.

01

Show Where Things Belong

Use words, pictures, symbols, or combinations that help children recognize where materials should be stored.

02

Make Materials Accessible

Place labels where children can see them and organize materials in ways that support appropriate independence.

03

Connect Words and Pictures

Visual supports can help children connect printed language with familiar objects, spaces, and routines.

04

Keep It Consistent

Consistent organization makes the classroom easier for children and adults to understand and navigate.
