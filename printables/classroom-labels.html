INTENTIONAL CLASSROOM DESIGN

Organization Can Support Independence

Classroom labels can help children recognize familiar spaces, locate materials, participate in cleanup, and understand where classroom resources belong.

01

Show Where Things Belong

Use labels to identify where materials and classroom resources are stored.

02

Make Materials Easier to Find

Clear organization helps children and adults navigate classroom materials.

03

Connect Words and Pictures

Combine familiar visuals with words to strengthen classroom organization.

04

Create Consistency

Consistent labels can make classroom systems easier to understand and use.
