Why Family Conferences Matter

Building a Partnership Around the Child

Family conferences create opportunities for teachers and families to share information, celebrate progress, discuss concerns, and work together to support each child's learning and development.

Celebrate Growth

Share children's accomplishments, strengths, interests, relationships, and developmental progress with the people who know them best.

Share Information

Create space for teachers and families to exchange observations, questions, experiences, and important information about the child.

Build Partnership

Use shared decision making and family knowledge to develop meaningful strategies and experiences that support children's continued growth.

Plan Next Steps

Document goals, strategies, family priorities, and intentional teaching opportunities that can guide continued learning after the conference.

Conference Process

From Preparation to Follow Up

A strong family conference is more than one conversation. These four steps help teachers organize information and keep the partnership moving forward.

1. Prepare

Review observations, work samples, developmental evidence, interests, strengths, and questions before meeting with the family.

2. Connect

Listen to the family's perspective, share classroom information, celebrate progress, and create space for meaningful conversation.

3. Plan

Identify shared priorities, goals, strategies, resources, and opportunities that can support the child's continued development.

4. Follow Up

Document decisions and return to the family's goals and questions through ongoing communication and continued observation.
