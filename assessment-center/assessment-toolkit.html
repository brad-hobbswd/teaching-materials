Practical Assessment

Use Assessment as an Ongoing Process

Strong assessment is more than completing a form. Use documentation throughout the year to understand children, plan intentionally, communicate with families, and reflect on learning.

Observe

Watch, listen, and notice what children are doing, saying, exploring, creating, and discovering during authentic classroom experiences.

Document

Record meaningful evidence through observations, work samples, photographs, developmental notes, and other classroom documentation.

Reflect

Review evidence to identify strengths, emerging skills, interests, developmental growth, and opportunities for intentional teaching.

Partner

Share meaningful information with families and use their knowledge, observations, and priorities to strengthen planning.
